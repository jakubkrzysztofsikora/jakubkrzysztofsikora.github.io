Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-15

Who operates this app

FamilyTube is a private application operated by a single household for its own members. The operator is an individual parent, not a company.

What data is processed

The app processes, on the family's own equipment:

  • Watch history of the family's own devices (used to recommend and filter content),
  • A parent-approved list of YouTube channels,
  • Google account data only if the family chooses to connect the family's own Google account to fetch its own subscriptions (see below).

Google / YouTube

FamilyTube can connect to a Google account chosen by the operator to read that account's YouTube subscriptions. When connected, Google provides YouTube data to the app. The app has no independent data-collection relationship with any advertiser and does not serve ads.

No selling, no third parties

No data is sold. No data is shared with advertisers or data brokers. The app talks to the family's own server over the household network; nothing is transmitted to any public server operated by a third party.

Retention and deletion

Data lives on the family's own hardware. The family can delete watch history and disconnect the connected Google account at any time.

Contact

Because this is a private family application, contact is limited to the household running it.
