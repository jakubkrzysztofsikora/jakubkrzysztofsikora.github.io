FamilyTube

Private family app — not a public service

What it is

FamilyTube is a small, self-hosted video player a single family runs for their own children. It plays a parent-approved YouTube feed on household devices (Apple TV, iPhone, iPad) and filters out unwanted channels.

What it is not

FamilyTube has no public registration, no public sign-up, no user accounts for the general public, and no cloud offering. It operates from the family's own home network and is not available for download by anyone outside that household.

How private

The app talks only to the family's own server. It exists to limit what children can watch, and no data is aggregated, sold, or shared with third parties beyond what the Google account the family itself chooses to connect provides.

This website exists solely to satisfy the identification and disclosure requirements of app-marketplace vendors. The software is not for commerce.
